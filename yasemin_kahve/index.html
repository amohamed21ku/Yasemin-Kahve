Yasemin Kahve

Yasemin Kahve is a Turkish green coffee importer and wholesale supplier of unroasted specialty coffee beans. Born in Guatemala and perfected in Türkiye since 1921, we supply roasters, distributors, coffee shops and restaurants in Türkiye and worldwide.

Yasemin Kahve operates under Olabi Holding, a family business rooted in the coffee world since 1951, with four generations of experience. We source from our own family-owned farms in Guatemala and from more than 15 origins across Latin America, Africa and Asia. Our team includes certified Q Graders working in a Q Venue accredited coffee laboratory, so every lot is cupped and graded before it ships.

What we supply

  • Wholesale green coffee: premium unroasted beans for roasters and distributors
  • Retail green coffee: smaller quantities of the same lots
  • Eversys coffee machines
  • Premium cardamom
